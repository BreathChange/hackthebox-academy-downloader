**📓 HackTheBox Academy Downloader** is the complete English-language guide to building a **personal offline archive of your own HackTheBox Academy study materials** — your notes, your lab write-ups, your solved flags, and your certificates. Learn how to structure a local study vault, export your own notes to Markdown, index them for search, and track module progress — with runnable Python and a clean folder layout. Built for HTB students, pentesters, and CTF players who want their own learning trail organized. **Completely free.** No signup. No paywall. No hidden chapters.

Most "HTB tools" posts are vague about what they actually do. **This guide is explicit: it archives *your own* study output** — notes, flags you solved, commands you used, certificates you earned — into a local, searchable Markdown vault. No scraping of paid content, no account sharing, no bypassing Academy access.
